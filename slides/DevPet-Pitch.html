DevPet - Your Gamified IDE Companion

Nurturing better coding habits, one line at a time.

Hello everyone! Today we're excited to introduce DevPet, your new gamified IDE companion. DevPet is designed to make coding more engaging and to nurture better coding habits, one line of code at a time.

The Problem

  • Developer Burnout: Coding can be a grind. Staring at screens all day leads to fatigue.
  • Poor Habits: Developers often neglect writing tests and Javadocs.
  • The AI/Copy-Paste Trap: Blindly pasting code or over-relying on AI can lead to unverified, buggy code.
Let's talk about the problem. Software development can be a real grind, often leading to burnout. When we're tired, we tend to skip writing tests and documentation. On top of that, with the rise of AI tools, it's incredibly easy to fall into the copy-paste trap — blindly pasting large chunks of unverified code, which can introduce subtle bugs and technical debt.

The Solution

  • Introducing DevPet: A virtual pet that lives right inside your IntelliJ Tool Window.
  • Core Concept: Your pet grows and thrives based on your actual coding activity. It turns the daily grind into a rewarding, gamified experience.
Our solution is DevPet. It's a virtual pet that lives directly inside your IntelliJ IDE. The core concept is simple: your pet grows and thrives based on your actual, human coding activity. By gamifying the development process, DevPet turns the daily grind into a rewarding and fun experience.

Key Features (The Mechanics)

  • Evolution: Egg → Duckling → Duck → Rubber Ducky.
  • Daily Goals: Set a daily LOC quota to keep your pet happy.
  • Quality over Quantity: Tests and Javadocs earn higher XP multipliers.
  • Sickness Mechanic: AI-heavy or messy code lowers health; write genuine code to recover.
How does it work? Your pet starts as an egg and evolves into a rubber ducky as you level up. You set a daily lines-of-code quota to keep it happy. But it's not just about quantity — quality matters! Writing tests and Javadocs gives you XP multipliers. And here's the twist: if you paste large chunks of code, your pet gets sick! The only way to cure it is to write genuine, human-typed code.

Gamification & Economy

  • DevCoins: Meeting your daily quota generates passive DevCoins over time.
  • The Shop: Spend coins on cosmetics — party hats, wizard hats, and backgrounds (Space, Forest, Ocean).
  • Achievements: Unlock badges like "Centurion", "Build Master", and "Rubber Ducky".
To keep things engaging, we've built an economy. Meeting your daily quota generates DevCoins over time. You can spend these coins in the shop to buy cosmetics — like a wizard hat or a cyberpunk background for your pet. You also unlock achievements for hitting milestones, like Build Master for successful builds or Centurion for your first 100 lines.

The Value Proposition

  • For Developers: Makes coding fun again, provides micro-breaks, and gives a sense of daily accomplishment.
  • For Teams/Companies: Subtly incentivizes best practices (tests, docs) and discourages mindless copy-pasting.
Why does this matter? For developers, DevPet brings joy back to coding. It provides necessary micro-breaks and a tangible sense of daily accomplishment. For teams and companies, it's a subtle, positive reinforcement tool. It naturally incentivizes writing tests and documentation while discouraging the mindless copy-pasting that leads to technical debt.
