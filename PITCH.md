# DevPet Pitch Presentation

## Slide 1: Title Slide
**Visual:** A cute graphic of the Rubber Ducky pet wearing a hat from the shop (e.g., a wizard hat or propeller cap) sitting inside an IDE tool window.

**Text:**
* **Title:** DevPet - Your Gamified IDE Companion
* **Subtitle:** Nurturing better coding habits, one line at a time.

**Speaker Notes:**
> "Hello everyone! Today we're excited to introduce DevPet, your new gamified IDE companion. DevPet is designed to make coding more engaging and to nurture better coding habits, one line of code at a time."

---

## Slide 2: The Problem
**Visual:** Split screen showing a tired developer on one side, and a messy, undocumented, AI-generated code snippet on the other.

**Text:**
* **Developer Burnout:** Coding can be a grind. Staring at screens all day leads to fatigue.
* **Poor Habits:** Developers often neglect writing tests and Javadocs.
* **The AI/Copy-Paste Trap:** Blindly pasting code or over-relying on AI can lead to unverified, buggy code.

**Speaker Notes:**
> "Let's talk about the problem. Software development can be a real grind, often leading to burnout. When we're tired, we tend to skip writing tests and documentation. On top of that, with the rise of AI tools, it's incredibly easy to fall into the 'copy-paste trap'—blindly pasting large chunks of unverified code, which can introduce subtle bugs and technical debt."

---

## Slide 3: The Solution
**Visual:** A clean screenshot of the IntelliJ IDE with the DevPet tool window open on the right, showing a happy duckling.

**Text:**
* **Introducing DevPet:** A virtual pet that lives right inside your IntelliJ Tool Window.
* **Core Concept:** Your pet grows and thrives based on your actual coding activity. It turns the daily grind into a rewarding, gamified experience.

**Speaker Notes:**
> "Our solution is DevPet. It's a virtual pet that lives directly inside your IntelliJ IDE. The core concept is simple: your pet grows and thrives based on your actual, human coding activity. By gamifying the development process, DevPet turns the daily grind into a rewarding and fun experience."

---

## Slide 4: Key Features (The Mechanics)
**Visual:** Four icons representing the mechanics: an evolution chart (egg to duck), a target/goal icon, a checkmark/doc icon, and a sick emoji face.

**Text:**
* **Evolution:** Starts as an Egg -> Duckling -> Duck -> Rubber Ducky.
* **Daily Goals:** Set a daily Lines of Code (LOC) quota to keep your pet happy.
* **Quality over Quantity:** Writing Tests and Javadocs yields higher XP multipliers.
* **The Sickness Mechanic:** Pasting large chunks of code or using AI generators makes your pet sick! You must write genuine code to nurse it back to health.

**Speaker Notes:**
> "How does it work? Your pet starts as an egg and evolves into a rubber ducky as you level up. You set a daily lines-of-code quota to keep it happy. But it's not just about quantity—quality matters! Writing tests and Javadocs gives you XP multipliers. And here's the twist: if you paste large chunks of code, your pet gets sick! The only way to cure it is to write genuine, human-typed code."

---

## Slide 5: Gamification & Economy
**Visual:** A mockup of the DevPet shop showing DevCoins and cosmetic items like hats and backgrounds. Badges for achievements are displayed below.

**Text:**

* **The Shop:** Spend DevCoins on cosmetics like Party Hats, Wizard Hats, or custom backgrounds (Space, Night City).
* **Achievements:** Unlock badges like "Centurion", "Build Master", and "Rubber Ducky" to show off your progress.

**Speaker Notes:**

You can spend these coins in the shop to buy cosmetics—like a wizard hat or a cyberpunk background for your pet. You also unlock achievements for hitting milestones, like 'Build Master' for successful builds or 'Centurion' for your first 100 lines."
